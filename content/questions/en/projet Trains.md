This project was completed as part of my studies at IUT of Computer Science in Montpellier, in collaboration with another student. It involved developing a **digital adaptation of the board game _Trains_** in **Java**.  
%
Here is the project workflow:  
- Modeling the game elements using **object-oriented programming** principles  
- Using **graph theory** to manage movements and connections on the board  
- Developing an **interactive graphical interface** with **JavaFX**
