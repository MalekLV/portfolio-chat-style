During my internship at **CHU de Montpellier**, I worked within the **cellular therapy unit**, in an environment that was mostly non-technical. My role consisted of supporting the team on IT and data management aspects.  
%
My main project focused on a **SQL data migration** from a **MariaDB database** to a **proprietary business software**. In this context, I was responsible for:  
- Analyzing the structure of the existing database and **normalizing the data**  
- Identifying, correcting, and managing **inconsistencies and errors** present  
- Designing and implementing a **reliable and secure data migration process** to the new software  
%
All of these tasks were performed using **PL/SQL**, with a high level of **rigor and reliability**, given the **highly sensitive nature of the health data**.
