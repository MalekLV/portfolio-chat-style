This application allows you to ask only **predefined questions** that have been written in advance by me.  
%0
To make navigation and content discovery easier, several features have been implemented:
- A **side menu** lets you explore the main topics of the application,  
- Some answers offer **related question suggestions** to dive deeper into a topic in one click,  
- When typing a question in the **search bar**, similar questions are automatically suggested,  
- The green **( + ) button**, located to the left of the search bar, displays all available questions; one click lets you ask a question directly,  
- You can **delete a message** by hovering over it and clicking the corresponding cross, allowing you to go back in the conversation.  
%1
If the available questions do not cover your needs or if this format does not suit you, feel free to contact me. I would be happy to discuss and explore certain topics further with you.
