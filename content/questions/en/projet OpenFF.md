This project was completed as part of my studies at IUT of Computer Science, Toulouse Paul Sabatier. It involved **transforming, optimizing, and exploiting** the relational database of **Open Food Facts** using an **Excel** file containing nutritional and ecological data.  
%
Here is the project workflow:  
- **Data cleaning and transformation** with **KNIME**, significantly reducing storage size  
- Creation of **interactive dashboards** in **Power BI** for **analysis** of nutritional information  
- Development of a **prediction model** in **KNIME** to estimate the nutritional quality of products and predict their **Nutri-Score**
