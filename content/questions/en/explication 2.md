This application is closer to a **FAQ search engine (Frequently Asked Questions)** than to an artificial intelligence in the strict sense.  
%
All questions and answers were **written manually** beforehand. I therefore invite you to navigate through this content via an **interactive interface**, inspired by the user experience of the ChatGPT application.  
%
This design choice gives me **greater freedom in crafting responses** (integration of images, links, etc.), while also providing a **lighter and more optimized solution**, consuming less disk space and, above all, less RAM. It also helps avoid **unexpected or incorrect answers** that a generative AI could produce.  
%
I am nonetheless aware of the **limitations of this approach**, particularly in terms of user experience: users cannot freely ask their own questions. To compensate for this, I implemented as many **search-support features** as possible, along with a **deliberately broad question catalog**, in order to maintain a sense of freedom while browsing.
