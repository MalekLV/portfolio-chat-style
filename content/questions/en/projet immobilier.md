This project was completed as part of my studies at IUT of Computer Science, Toulouse Paul Sabatier, within a **team of 5 developers**. It involved designing a **real estate management application in Java** for a private client, to facilitate tracking of rentals, tenants, and expenses.  
%
Here is the project workflow:  
- **Design and modeling** of a **SQL relational database** from Excel files  
- Use of **PL/SQL** to automate calculations and produce statistics  
- Development of a **graphical interface** with **JavaFX / SceneBuilder**  
- Implementation of a **DAO architecture** and database connection via **JDBC** (programming in **Java** with **IntelliJ IDEA**)  
%
The entire project was executed following the **Agile 'Scrum' methodology** (working in sprints, task allocation…)
