This project was completed as part of my studies at IUT of Computer Science, Toulouse Paul Sabatier. It involved designing a **website with API** for **managing a volleyball team**, in collaboration with another student.  
%
Here is the project workflow:  
- **Design and modeling** of a **MySQL database** (players, users, matches)  
- Development of the **back-end in PHP** following **REST API** principles  
- Implementation of a **secure authentication system** (hashed passwords, tokens)  
- Implementation of **API routes** (GET, POST, DELETE…) for data management  
- Development of the **web interface** in **HTML / CSS** for viewing information and statistics
